Resource Categories

What's covered.

Every resource on the platform is verified by our team before it goes live — and re-checked on a regular schedule, because a wrong number in a crisis is worse than no number at all.

01

Emergency & Crisis

When seconds count. Emergency shelters, disaster response contacts, county emergency management offices, and preparedness guides — cached on your device so they work even when the network doesn't.

02

Housing & Shelter

Emergency housing assistance, shelters, transitional housing, and eviction-prevention resources. If you or someone you love needs a safe place tonight, this is where the platform starts.

03

Mental & Behavioral Health

Counseling services, crisis intervention, substance-use support, and peer programs — plus the always-available lines: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and 2-1-1 NJ Community Resources.

04

Medical & Health

Clinics, urgent care, prescription assistance, and community health centers, including low-cost and no-cost options for residents without insurance.

05

Food & Essentials

Food banks, pantries, soup kitchens, and emergency supply programs across the state — because stability starts with the basics.

06

Workforce & Job Training

Job training programs, employment services, and career resources that help residents move from crisis to stability — including our free virtual workshops on navigating training and certification pathways.

Are you a Resource Provider?

If you are a shelter, clinic, counselor, or community organization serving New Jersey residents, join our network of Verified Resource Providers and put your services in front of the residents who need them. Visit our Contact page to get started.
