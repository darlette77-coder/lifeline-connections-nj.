About Us

Connecting People to Critical Lifeline Connections.

Lifeline Connections NJ, Inc. is a New Jersey nonprofit building a unified digital infrastructure for public safety and community resilience.

Why we exist

In an emergency, the hardest question is also the simplest: where do I go, and who do I call? Across New Jersey, that answer is scattered across dozens of websites, outdated lists, and phone numbers that no longer work. When the power is out and the network is down, most of it disappears entirely.

Lifeline Connections NJ, Inc. was founded to close that gap. Our platform puts verified emergency protocols, housing resources, counseling, and medical assets within reach of every New Jersey resident — never more than three taps away — and it keeps working through power outages and network failures, because that is exactly when people need it most.

Who we are

Lifeline Connections NJ, Inc. is a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, registered with the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs. We are led by our Founder and President, Darlette Johnson, and guided by a volunteer Board of Trustees.

Our work is powered by donations and by our growing network of Verified Resource Providers — the shelters, clinics, counselors, and community organizations whose services make up the platform.
