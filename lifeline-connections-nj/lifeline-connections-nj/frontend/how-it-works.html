How It Works

Three taps to help.

From the moment you open Lifeline Connections NJ, you are never more than three taps away from the help you need.

Three steps

01

Enter your zip code

Type any New Jersey zip code into the search on our homepage. The platform instantly shows the verified resources closest to you — shelters, crisis lines, counseling, medical care, and more.

02

Get verified answers, not search results

Every listing is a Verified Resource Provider: a real organization with a checked address, a working phone number, and confirmed services. No dead links. No numbers that ring out. Just help that answers.

03

Save it to your home screen

Add Lifeline Connections NJ to your phone's home screen and it installs like an app — no app store, no download, no account. It opens faster, caches what you need, and works offline, so your resources stay available through power outages and network failures.

Built for the worst day, useful every day

Emergency preparedness guides live on the platform and are cached on your device — go-bag checklists, shelter planning, county alert sign-ups, and power-loss planning, available even with no signal.

Weather alerts and public safety notices keep you current on conditions in your area.

Always-on crisis lines are one tap away, any hour: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 1-800-572-SAFE (NJ Domestic Violence Hotline), 2-1-1 (NJ Community Resources), and 1-800-222-1222 (Poison Control).

Free for every New Jersey resident

Lifeline Connections NJ, Inc. is a nonprofit. The platform costs you nothing — donations keep the resource data verified, the alerts running, and access free for the people most likely to need it when it matters most.
